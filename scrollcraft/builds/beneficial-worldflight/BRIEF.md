# BRIEF — beneficial.technology landing (worldflight)

Interviewed. Verbatim user answers. Locked ahead of the interview: premium-minimal
(restrained), dark, a generated cinematic world via the user's KIE key, and the
page **replaces the current Next.js homepage**.

---

## 1. What it is / who for
Beneficial Technology is the layer between a founder's legal counsel and their
operating team. It designs the structure, systems, and software that pre-seed to
Series A founders need when they're building where the standard legal playbook
doesn't fit: token launches, climate assets, regulated AI, and cross-jurisdiction
setups.

## 2. Vibe
**Precise, quiet, load-bearing, legible.**
- **Salk Institute (Louis Kahn).** Concrete, light, one axis. Rigor that feels
  calm rather than cold.
- **Joy Division, *Unknown Pleasures* cover.** Raw data rendered as an image.
  Measurement that becomes beautiful without decoration.
- **Mini Metro (game).** A system that starts simple, grows complex, and stays
  legible the whole time. That's the job.

## 3. Scroll journey (their words)
1. Open on one line in an empty field: "If your legal questions keep coming back
   'it depends,' you're in the right place." Nothing else on screen.
2. **The gap.** Two structures appear: counsel on one side, the operating team on
   the other. They don't connect. The space between them is where token launches
   stall, raises slip, and structures get rebuilt after the fact.
3. **The missing layer.** The space fills in. Six practices lock into place as
   components of one structure, not a services menu.
4. **What gets built.** Concrete artifacts: multi-entity structures, SAFT and
   SAFE-T instruments, compliance architecture, investor narrative, production
   software. Shown as outputs, not capabilities.
5. **Who it's for.** Five founder profiles. The visitor should find themselves in
   one.
6. **How it works.** Tiers, stated plainly, with prices visible. No "contact us
   for pricing."
7. **The posture.** Not a law firm. We build what your counsel reviews and your
   team runs. One sentence, given real weight.
8. **Close.** The finished structure, seen whole for the first time, then the
   action.

## 4. Energy curve
Calm at both ends, intense in the middle. The open is nearly silent. Tension
builds through the gap and peaks as the layer assembles (stages 2 and 3). It
decelerates through what gets built and who it's for, then goes flat and still for
pricing and the close. Money and commitment get the calmest treatment on the
page, because that's where trust gets decided.

## 5. Feeling by stage
- Open: **recognition.** "That's literally my problem."
- The gap: **unease.** The cost is real, and I'm currently paying it.
- The layer: **relief and clarity.** Someone owns the middle.
- What gets built: **confidence.** They've done this specifically, not generally.
- Who it's for: **belonging.**
- Tiers and posture: **trust.** Plain numbers, honest limits.
- Close: **readiness.**

## 6. Belief and action
- **Belief:** "My legal structure and my operating system are one design problem,
  and someone should own the seam."
- **Action:** a 30-minute scoping call. **Label (used everywhere): "Book a
  scoping call".**

## 7. Signature move — "Depends On"
The visitor picks a question they've been told "it depends" on
("Is our token a security?", "Do we need a Cayman foundation?", "Can we sell
carbon credits on-chain?"). The page unfolds the **dependency tree** behind the
answer: the 4–5 variables it actually turns on, which the founder controls, which
require counsel. "It depends" becomes a legible map. Ends with "Want yours
resolved? Book a scoping call."

Why no one else does it: no other site treats **ambiguity as the product**. It
**demonstrates** the service instead of describing it.

**UPL failure mode to design around (hard constraint):** biggest unauthorized-
practice-of-law exposure on the site. Trees are **hand-built and fixed** (6–8
canonical questions), **never live AI answers**. They show **variables and
structure only, never conclusions**, and **every tree carries a visible
counsel-review marker**. **Tyler signs off on each tree before it ships.**

## 8. Structural fork — ONE UNBROKEN WORLD (worldflight)
The thesis is that legal, technical, and operating decisions are one system.
Chapters would reproduce the silos BT removes. The scroll moves through a single
architecture that assembles as you go: two disconnected masses → a middle layer
builds between them → a final zoom-out shows the whole structure for the first
time.

**Performance failure mode (hard constraint):** a continuous 3D world is
expensive and fragile on mobile, and BT *sells* Core Web Vitals / LCP
optimization, so a slow hero would be self-refuting. **Performance budget set
before design. Static, reduced-motion fallback that still tells the full story.**

## The feeling curve (one line per leg; cause on screen)
- **L1 Surface / Open** — recognition. One line in an empty concrete field; the
  camera barely breathes.
- **L2 The gap** — unease. Two separated masses; the void between them widens as
  the cost is named.
- **L3 The layer assembles** — relief → **PEAK**. The middle fills; six members
  lock into the span between the two masses. Largest scroll span, silence before.
- **L4 What gets built** — confidence. The camera moves along the finished span;
  concrete artifacts read as outputs.
- **L5 Who it's for** — belonging. Five bays along the structure; the visitor
  finds their own.
- **L6 Tiers + posture** — trust. The world goes flat and still; plain numbers,
  the "not a law firm" sentence with weight.
- **L7 Close / whole** — readiness. Zoom-out to the whole structure for the first
  time, then the action.

## The peak
**"You scroll and the empty gap in the middle actually builds itself into the
structure — the thing they say owns 'the seam', you watch get built."** Lives in
**L3**. It gets the largest scroll span and the quiet of L2 before it.

## Tell-someone sentence
**"It's the site where it takes your own 'it depends' legal question apart and
shows you the map underneath."**

## Authored silences (not dead scroll)
- The **open (L1)** is deliberately near-silent: one line, empty field, minimal
  camera. Intended.
- The **run into pricing (L6)** deliberately flattens and stills — money gets the
  calmest treatment. Intended.
