# "Depends On" — canonical trees for sign-off

**UPL guardrails (built into every tree):**
- Hand-built, fixed. **No live AI. No generated answers.**
- Shows **the variables the question turns on, and who owns each** — never a
  conclusion, recommendation, or "yes/no."
- Every tree ends with a **visible counsel-review marker** and the one action.
- **Nothing ships until Tyler signs off on each tree below.**

Legend per variable: **[You]** = founder-controlled input · **[Counsel]** =
requires a lawyer's analysis · **[Facts]** = objective facts to gather.

Standardized footer on every tree:
> This is a map of what the answer depends on — not the answer. Structure only,
> reviewed by counsel. **Want yours resolved? Book a scoping call.**

---

### 1. "Is our token a security?"
Turns on:
- **How the token is sold and marketed** — expectations set with buyers. [You]
- **What the token does at launch vs. later** — live utility vs. promised future. [You/Facts]
- **How decentralized the network/effort is** — reliance on a core team. [Facts]
- **Where buyers are and how they're reached** — jurisdictions, channels. [You]
- **Which legal framework(s) apply** to the above pattern. [Counsel]

### 2. "Do we need a Cayman (or offshore) foundation?"
Turns on:
- **What the entity needs to hold or do** — token issuance, treasury, governance. [You]
- **Where your team, buyers, and operations sit** — jurisdictional footprint. [Facts]
- **Tax posture of the founders and the entity** — residency, flow-through. [Counsel]
- **Governance model** — foundation vs. company vs. DAO wrapper. [You/Counsel]
- **Banking, audit, and ongoing admin you can sustain.** [You]

### 3. "Can we sell carbon credits on-chain?"
Turns on:
- **What the credit actually is** — registry-issued, methodology, vintage. [Facts]
- **Registry rules on tokenization/transfer** — whether wrapping is permitted. [Facts]
- **How the on-chain token maps to the underlying credit** — 1:1, custody, retirement. [You]
- **Commodity / securities characterization** of the on-chain instrument. [Counsel]
- **Buyer location and marketing** — cross-border transfer rules. [You/Counsel]

### 4. "Does our AI feature trigger regulated-activity obligations?"
Turns on:
- **What decision the AI influences** — credit, employment, health, legal, safety. [You]
- **Sector rules for that decision** — the regulated domain it touches. [Counsel]
- **Data the model uses** — personal, sensitive, cross-border. [Facts]
- **Degree of human oversight in the loop.** [You]
- **Jurisdictions of users** — differing AI/data regimes. [Facts]

### 5. "Can US persons participate in our token sale or airdrop?"
Turns on:
- **The token's characterization** (see #1). [Counsel]
- **The distribution mechanism** — sale, airdrop, rewards, lockups. [You]
- **Eligibility controls you can enforce** — KYC, geoblock, accreditation. [You]
- **What participants receive and when** — immediate vs. vesting. [You]
- **Exemptions or registration paths** that fit the pattern. [Counsel]

### 6. "Which entity should hold our IP and sign customer contracts?"
Turns on:
- **Where the IP is created and by whom** — assignment chain. [Facts]
- **Who the customer is and where** — contracting-entity footprint. [You]
- **Token/foundation vs. operating-company split** — what sits where. [You/Counsel]
- **Tax and liability consequences** of the chosen holder. [Counsel]
- **Investor and diligence expectations** on the IP's home. [You/Counsel]
